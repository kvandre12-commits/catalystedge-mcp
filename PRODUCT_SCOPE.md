# CatalystEdge pre-midterm MVP scope

Target window: before the November 3, 2026 U.S. midterm election.

## Customer job

Explain why a user-supplied market level is testing, rejecting, failing, or being
accepted, while placing that behavior inside a verified catalyst window.

## MVP outputs

- exact level and direction under test;
- state: no_test, testing, provisional, rejected, accepted_unconfirmed, confirmed;
- close-acceptance evidence;
- breach extension;
- held-retest evidence;
- volume-participation evidence when available;
- candle-displacement evidence;
- catalyst context as context, never confirmation;
- explicit missing evidence and limitations.

## Launch boundaries

- No autonomous trading or broker connection.
- No claim that a heuristic is a calibrated probability.
- No options recommendation without implied move, IV, liquidity, expiry, and max-loss inputs.
- No dependence on Yahoo or Cboe redistribution for the public MVP.
- User-supplied bars and levels first; official event schedules added behind source and freshness receipts.
- Earnings dates must be issuer-confirmed or labeled unverified.

## Why a line breaks

A line breaks for product purposes only when the auction demonstrates acceptance,
not because price printed one tick beyond it. CatalystEdge requires repeated closes
and at least one confirming behavior: participation expansion, decisive displacement,
or a retest that holds. A catalyst can explain why participation may arrive, but it
cannot replace those observations.
